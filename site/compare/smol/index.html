Dinky vs Smol

Smol is a paid menu-bar app with Smart Clipboard and on-device AI upscaling. Dinky is free, open source, about 35 MB—and covers audio alongside stills, MP4, and PDF in the same drop zone.

Choose Smol for menu bar and Smart Clipboard

When the menu bar is home base, you want automatic clipboard compression without a hotkey, you need RAW camera files or on-device AI upscaling, and a one-time purchase with trial is fine.

Choose Dinky for free OSS, audio, and automation

When you want inspectable source, no purchase, watch folders included, deeper PDF paths (flatten, preserve, optional OCR), audio cross-converts, CLI and dinky serve, URL import—and the smallest all-in-one install.
